The experience we want to build: matrimony with more heart.

By Favorite Hearts

Finding a person to build a life with deserves a thoughtful experience. We are shaping Favorite Hearts around meaningful introductions, honest conversations, and the everyday realities of marriage.

Start with intention

Our product vision begins with people who are considering marriage. We want the experience to make room for personality, practical expectations, and the future each person hopes to build.

Make the important things clear

A thoughtful profile should help someone understand who is behind it, what matters to the candidate, and what kind of partnership they are seeking. Clarity matters when a family member helps manage an introduction.

Keep people at the centre

These principles guide the experience we are developing. We will share specific feature announcements here as they are ready.

In brief

We are building a matrimony experience around clear intentions, honest profiles, and meaningful introductions. Our goal is to help people understand one another and the future they hope to share, with clarity about any family member’s role.
