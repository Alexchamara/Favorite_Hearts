A thoughtful introduction starts with an honest profile.

By Favorite Hearts

A profile is a starting point for a conversation. It does not need to tell your whole story or make your life look perfect. It should give someone an honest glimpse of the person behind it.

Describe your actual life

Write about a few things that matter to you, what you enjoy, and the future you hope for. Specific, honest details give another person something to ask about.

Be clear about intentions

Say what you are looking for in a partnership. If a parent or another person manages the profile, make that role clear and keep the candidate’s wishes central.

Share with care

Being open does not require publishing private contact or identity details. Save sensitive information for an appropriate stage, and only share what you feel comfortable sharing.

In brief

An honest profile offers a clear glimpse of your life and intentions. Share specific details, make any profile manager’s role clear, and keep the candidate’s wishes central. Being open does not require sharing sensitive personal information.
