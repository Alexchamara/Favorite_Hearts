The little things worth talking about before marriage.

By Favorite Hearts

A wedding is one occasion. A marriage is made up of ordinary days. Conversations about those days can help two people understand what partnership would mean to them.

Think beyond the big occasion

Discuss everyday responsibilities, time alone, friendships, and how you like to spend weekends. These details make the idea of a shared life more concrete.

Make room for differences

One person may need time to think; another may want to talk immediately. Ask how each of you prefers to approach a disagreement instead of expecting the same style.

Keep the conversation open

You will not settle every question in one sitting. Return to the topics that matter as you get to know each other, without rushing a decision.

In brief

Talk about the ordinary days as well as the wedding: responsibilities, friendships, time alone, and how you handle disagreements. You do not need every answer immediately. Keep learning about one another without rushing the decision.
