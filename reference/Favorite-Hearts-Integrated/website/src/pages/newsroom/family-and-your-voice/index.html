Family can be part of the journey. Your voice comes first.

By Favorite Hearts

Family can bring warmth and perspective to an introduction. The people considering marriage still need space to discover what they want, in their own words and at their own pace.

Share the support you want

Let your family know what would help: finding introductions, thinking through questions, or simply listening. Be clear about which decisions you want to make yourself.

Keep expectations visible

Talk about timelines and preferences before an introduction. If expectations differ, try explaining your reasons rather than only stating a rule.

Keep consent at the centre

Family enthusiasm is not a substitute for your own willingness. A decision about marriage should leave both people free to say yes, no, or that they need more time.

In brief

Family support can be valuable, but your voice and willingness come first. Explain the support you want, discuss expectations openly, and protect both people’s freedom to say yes, no, or ask for more time.
