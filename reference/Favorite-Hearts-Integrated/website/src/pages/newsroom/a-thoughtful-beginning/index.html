A shared future starts with a thoughtful beginning.

By Favorite Hearts

A happy family begins long before a wedding. It begins with two people having the space to understand one another, choose freely, and imagine a future they both want. That is the idea at the heart of Favorite Hearts.

Our purpose

Favorite Hearts is being built around a simple hope: to make life less lonely by bringing people together to build happy families. Our focus is matrimony for Sri Lankans at home and abroad.

A choice that belongs to you

Marriage is a personal decision. Family support can be valuable, but an introduction should always leave room for the wishes, boundaries, and pace of the people involved.

Get to know us

Our Mission page shares the values behind this vision. Favorite Labs explores the questions we want to ask about shared values and lasting partnerships.

In brief

Favorite Hearts aims to make life less lonely by helping people build happy families. Our approach to matrimony puts personal choice first, welcomes thoughtful family support, and makes space for each person’s boundaries and pace.
