Across borders, on the same page.

By Favorite Hearts

A connection across countries can begin with shared roots. Building a future together also means talking openly about distance, expectations, and where each of you hopes life will lead.

Discuss what home means

Ask where each person hopes to live and how close they want to be to family. Do not assume that a move abroad or a return to Sri Lanka is part of the plan.

Talk about the practical details

Time zones, travel, careers, and caring responsibilities deserve a place in the conversation. It is fine to have questions that cannot be answered immediately.

Build a realistic next step

Agree on a way to stay in touch that works for both people. If you plan to meet, choose a public place and keep someone you trust informed.

In brief

A shared future across countries needs honest conversations about home, careers, travel, and family responsibilities. Avoid assumptions about relocation, agree on a realistic way to stay in touch, and plan any first meeting with care.
