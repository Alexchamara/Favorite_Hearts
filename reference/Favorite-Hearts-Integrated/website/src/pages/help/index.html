Getting started

What is Favorite Hearts?+

Favorite Hearts is a matrimony platform being built for Sri Lankans at home and around the world. Our mission is to make life less lonely by bringing people together to build happy families.

Who is Favorite Hearts for?+

Favorite Hearts is for adults aged 18 and over who are considering marriage. Your own wishes, boundaries, and readiness should guide the journey.

Can I create an account on this website?+

Account registration is not available on this website yet. You can explore our approach, read our articles, and contact our team with questions about getting started.

Can I join if I live outside Sri Lanka?+

Our vision includes Sri Lankans living abroad as well as those in Sri Lanka. Location, plans for the future, and family expectations are useful things to discuss early.

Profiles & family

Can a parent or family member create a profile?+

The planned matrimony experience supports profiles created by a candidate or an authorised parent, guardian, or other manager. The manager’s role should be clear, and the candidate’s wishes should stay central.

What should I include in a profile?+

Share an honest introduction, your interests, and what you hope for in a partnership. Details about education, work, and everyday life can help someone start a meaningful conversation.

Which photos should I choose?+

Choose recent, clear photos that represent you. Use photos you have permission to share, and avoid including other people’s private information or images without their consent.

How do I get help with profile changes?+

Contact our team with a short description of what you want to change. Avoid sending identity documents, passwords, or one-time codes in your first message.

Does a family introduction mean I have to continue?+

No. An introduction is an invitation. Both people should feel free to ask for time, set boundaries, or decide that the connection is not right for them.

Introductions

How will an interest request work?+

In the planned matrimony journey, one person sends an interest request and the other chooses whether to accept. Mutual acceptance is the next step towards getting to know each other.

Will there be chat inside matrimony?+

The planned matrimony experience does not include in-app chat. Contact details are intended to become available after mutual acceptance, so both people can decide how to continue.

When will private photos and contact details be shared?+

The planned experience keeps sensitive profile details and the full photo gallery private until both sides accept an interest request. Always consider what you feel comfortable sharing.

What should we talk about first?+

Begin with everyday life, what matters to each of you, and the future you hope to build. You do not need to settle every important question in the first conversation.

Payments

Do I need to pay to publish a profile?+

The planned matrimony service requires a paid subscription to publish a profile, for both men and women. Final plans and prices will be shown before you make a payment.

How will bank-transfer payments work?+

The planned flow is to choose a plan, follow the payment instructions, and submit proof of payment for review. A subscription becomes active after approval. This website does not currently accept payments.

Can I ask about a payment or payment slip?+

Yes. Contact support with your question and the relevant payment reference, if you have one. Do not share passwords, one-time codes, card security codes, or full bank statements.

Where can I find pricing and refund information?+

Our pricing page lists proposed packages in LKR and USD. Plan duration, final benefits, and refund terms must be confirmed before payment. Ask our team for clarification; do not rely on prices in social comments or messages from other users.

Privacy & safety

How can I protect my personal information?+

Keep passwords, one-time codes, identity documents, financial information, and your home address private. Avoid posting sensitive details in a profile or public comment.

How do I raise a safety concern?+

Contact us on WhatsApp or by email with a short description of the concern. Keep relevant messages or screenshots, but avoid sharing unnecessary personal information. You can stop an uncomfortable interaction at any time.

What if someone asks me for money?+

Do not send money to someone you have only met online. Stop and seek support if a person pressures you to pay, invest, share bank details, or keep financial requests secret.

How should I prepare for a first meeting?+

Choose a public place, arrange your own transport, and tell someone you trust where you are going. Meet at a pace you feel comfortable with and leave if you feel uneasy.

How can I ask about my data or account?+

Email hello@favoritehearts.com with a brief description of your request. Our team can explain the appropriate next step. Never include passwords or one-time codes in your message.

NFAQ

Not-so frequently asked questions

What does NFAQ mean?+

Not-so frequently asked questions: the things you might be wondering about, even if they do not come up in every conversation.

Do we need to agree on everything?+

No two people are identical. What matters is having room to talk honestly about differences, understand each other’s priorities, and decide what you can build together.

What if my family and I want different things?+

Try explaining what matters to you and the kind of support you would welcome. Family can offer perspective, but your own willingness should never be replaced by pressure.

Does Favorite Hearts guarantee a marriage?+

No. A meaningful partnership depends on the people involved. Introductions can create opportunities, but neither a profile nor a subscription can guarantee a relationship or marriage.

Is horoscope matching available?+

Horoscope matching is not available on this website. It is reserved for a later version of the product.

When can I contact the team?+

Our listed support hours are daily, 8:00 AM–10:00 PM, Sri Lanka time. You can send a WhatsApp message at any time; replies are handled during support hours. Favorite Hearts is not an emergency service.
